## Platform

web

## Users

(inferred) Owners of small, independent fitness studios: yoga, pilates, climbing gyms. They run the business themselves, often also teach, and today juggle spreadsheets, Instagram DMs and a card reader to fill classes and take payment.

## Product Purpose

(inferred) Mooring is a booking and scheduling tool. It handles bookings, waitlists and payments for a studio so the owner can get back on the mat. Success: classes fill without the owner answering messages.

## Positioning

(inferred) Built for the owner-operator of one to five small studios, not chains. The mechanism: a single booking link (bio, site, poster at the door) that takes booking and payment in two taps, with an automatic waitlist that hands a cancelled spot to the next person and notifies them.

## Operating Context

(inferred) Owners set up a weekly class schedule (often imported from a spreadsheet), share one booking link, then teach. Members book from a phone browser, usually from Instagram. Payments run through Stripe or Square with payouts to the studio's own account.

## Capabilities and Constraints

(from brief) Online booking; automatic waitlists with notification; memberships, class packs, drop-ins in one checkout; CSV member import; email reminders (Studio), SMS reminders, staff accounts and up to five locations (Collective). Monthly plans, no contract. Browser booking, optional app for regulars.

## Brand Commitments

(from brief) Name: Mooring. Copy in brief.md is verbatim and fixed. No external images or stock photography.

## Evidence on Hand

(from brief) Claims supplied by the brief and usable as-is: 1,200 independent studios; 3.4M classes booked; 38% fewer no-shows; 6 hours saved per week; one testimonial from Ana Reyes, owner of Low Tide Yoga. Pricing: Studio $39/month, Collective $89/month. No other customers, logos, metrics or testimonials exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. The owner's time goes to teaching, not admin.
2. One link does the whole job for the member.
3. Plain, honest terms: monthly, cancel any time, money lands in the studio's account.
