## Platform

## Users

Owner-operators of small fitness studios (yoga, pilates, climbing gyms) who run the business themselves. Today they juggle spreadsheets, Instagram DMs and a card reader, usually from a phone at the front desk between classes.

## Product Purpose

Mooring handles bookings, waitlists and payments so classes fill without the owner answering messages. Success: full classes, fewer no-shows, hours back each week.

## Positioning

A class schedule that refills itself: when someone cancels, the next person on the waitlist gets the spot and a notification automatically.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Online booking and payment from a link, site or Instagram; automatic waitlists; class packs, memberships, drop-ins in one checkout; CSV member import; Stripe and Square payouts; browser booking with optional app. Plans: Studio $39/month, Collective $89/month. 14-day free trial, no card.
