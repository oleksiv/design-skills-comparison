# Surface brief: Mooring landing page

Scope: app/impeccable/page.tsx, the Mooring marketing landing page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: owner-operators of small yoga, pilates and climbing studios. Action: Start free trial. Proof: the automatic waitlist, shown working; brief-supplied stats and one testimonial. Constraints: brief.md copy verbatim and in order; Tailwind utilities only; no raster or external imagery; 390 to 1440px.

Decision round: unattended subagent run, no user. The decision page was not served; the assigned direction is built. Challenger verdicts: plankton wake, neon circuit, seven-segment, shader portal, painted poster all declined; festival lineup competitive on product clarity, not built.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The studio's class is a Pilates reformer's spring bar: every spot is a spring, booked spots hook on and pull taut, open spots are empty hooks. Refuses the sage-and-beige wellness page with a phone mockup and three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Aubergine stitched upholstery as the committed field (hero, quote, close), brushed aluminium rails as the light ground, springs in reformer tension colours red, yellow, green, blue used only for engaged state; rest stays aluminium grey. Archivo across widths: expanded heavy nameplate display, normal body, condensed caps for stamped labels. Pill-rounded rails, padded panels with an inset stitch line.

STORY: The owner sees a class filling itself, believes the waitlist does the chasing, starts a free trial.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Aluminium header rail. A full-width padded upholstery carriage holds the headline at nameplate scale, subheadline, both buttons and the note. Bolted beneath, full width, the spring bar: one example class, twelve hooks, most sprung, waitlist beside.

FORM: Pilates reformer spring bar, position 5 of 7, seed:0321df210f0e011d. Signature interaction: tap an open hook to book, cancel a spring and the first waitlisted name hooks on with a notice. Motion: spring stretch, eased out, once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Raises from declined challengers:
- Raised by plankton wake: colour only marks engaged state; everything at rest returns to aluminium.
- Raised by seven-segment: absence is drawn as deliberately as presence; empty hooks are designed objects, not blanks.
- Raised by shader portal: one ground hue committed at full page scale, not an accent.
- Raised by painted poster: size is billing; the headline and the three stats outrank everything by scale alone.
- Raised by neon circuit: every spring and hook obeys one geometry (pitch, radius, hook size) everywhere it appears.

Unresolved: none for this run.
